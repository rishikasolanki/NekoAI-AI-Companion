NekoAI is an AI-powered desktop productivity companion designed to help users build better habits, manage tasks, stay organized, and achieve their personal and academic goals. Unlike traditional productivity applications, NekoAI combines a persistent virtual pet with intelligent mentoring to make productivity more interactive, engaging, and personalized.

Powered by Google's Gemini AI, NekoAI provides context-aware conversations, personalized guidance, and AI-assisted daily planning based on tasks, habits, available time, and user memories. Its virtual pet delivers task reminders and interactive reactions, while the habit-tracking system monitors progress, streaks, and consistency. The application also includes task management, a daily planner, long-term memory, chat-based mentoring, and productivity analytics.

Built using React, FastAPI, Electron, and SQLite, NekoAI integrates AI capabilities with a desktop experience to help users overcome procrastination, maintain consistency, and turn their goals into achievable daily actions.

NekoAI's vision is simple: to transform productivity from a list of responsibilities into a personalized journey with an intelligent companion by your side.
